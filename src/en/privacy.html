Privacy policy

The tools process images inside the browser on your device. They do not upload your files to a server or an external image-processing service, and do not send image contents for analytics or advertising. Images remain in page memory during use; closing or reloading the page ends the session. Downloaded PDFs remain wherever your device saves them.

Optional saved preferences

Saving is off by default. If you choose Remember print settings, values such as paper size and margins are stored in LocalStorage on your device. Images and file names are not saved there. Use Clear saved preferences in any tool to remove these settings. Your browser may block local storage.

Hosting data is different from your images

Loading this site from Cloudflare exposes technical connection information such as your IP address, request time and browser information to the hosting provider. Cloudflare may process security events and logs under its policies. This is separate from images you select locally. The site uses Google AdSense on its home page and informational pages. Advertising scripts are excluded from the three image tools, the ruler test and error pages. The site does not include a separate analytics service.

Google advertising and consent

Google and its partners may use cookies, identifiers and connection data to serve and measure ads, depending on the configuration and required consent. The site operator has published a consent message through Google AdSense Privacy & messaging for applicable European regions. Where this message is shown, use its consent, refusal and management options to make your choices. Its availability and language depend on Google settings and your region. The site does not replace Google's message with its own consent popup. See Google's explanation of advertising technologies.

Your choices and contact

You can use the tools without saving settings. No contact form sends your information from this site. For privacy questions, use the email address on the contact page. If you email, the message and sender details are handled by the relevant email providers; do not include sensitive images.
