Tool 02 · Exact photo size

Print a photo at the size you need.

Enter a frame width and height in centimeters or millimeters. Keep the complete image or crop it to fill, with a preview before you print.

1. Images

Choose Edit image to crop, rotate or change pixel dimensions.

Supported files and limits

JPG, PNG and WebP. Up to 30 images, 20 MiB per file and 12 megapixels per image. Total originals and edits: 32 megapixels and 80 MiB. Decoded pixels alone may use 128 MB, plus preview and export memory. Maximum 30 PDF pages.

    Short guide and examples

    How do I set print dimensions?

    Choose a preset or enter width and height in cm or mm. Link dimensions to keep the image ratio. The frame must fit inside the page margins.

    What is Fit versus Crop?

    Fit keeps the whole picture and may leave white space. Crop fills the frame by trimming edges without stretching. Use Edit image first if you want to choose which part to keep.

    Quick example

    For a 10 × 15 cm frame, choose that preset and compare Fit and Crop. Print the PDF at 100% or Actual size, and use the ruler test to check your printer settings.
